About CloudPeak Solutions

We're a small team of AWS-certified engineers who've spent the last decade helping companies build reliable, cost-effective cloud infrastructure.

Our Story

CloudPeak Solutions was founded to close a gap we kept seeing: businesses adopting AWS without a clear architecture strategy, leading to runaway costs and fragile systems. We started as a two-person consultancy helping local startups migrate off legacy hosting, and have since grown into a full-service AWS partner working with companies of all sizes.

Today, our focus hasn't changed: build infrastructure that's secure, scalable, and understandable by the team that has to maintain it.

Our Mission

To make enterprise-grade cloud architecture accessible to businesses of every size — without the jargon, over-engineering, or surprise bills.
